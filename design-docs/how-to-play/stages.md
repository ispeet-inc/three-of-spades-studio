#### Key points - 

1. Trump beats other suites
2. Teammate is only revealed when teammate card is played.
3. Bid winner must score at least their bid amount along with their teammate.

#### Bidding phase

A randomly chosen player X, starts bidding with 165 points

In their turn, each player can either bid higher or pass. 

Turn moves in clockwise fashion
Bidding continues until only one player remains
The bid winner gets to choose the trump suite & teammate card. 



#### Trump Selection

The bidder chooses a Trump Suite.
The bidder selects a Teammate Card (a card they don't have in their hand)

Team Formation: The player who has the teammate card becomes the bidder's partner
Hidden Teams: Teams are not revealed until the teammate card is played



#### Playing Phase

Player X starts the first trick.

Trick lifecycle

- Trick start: Play any card of your choice from your hand. The suite of this card becomes the running suite.
- Subsequent players need to play a card from the running suite if they have one. In case, they don't they can cut the round with a card from the trump suite. (Trump suite >> any other suite)
- Players play cards in clockwise fashion till each player has played their card.
- Winning: Highest trump wins, or highest card of the running suite if no trump is played.
- Trick Collection: The winner collects the trick cards and starts the next round. The points from the cards played in the trick go to the winner.



#### Scoring Phase

Once all 10 tricks are completed. If bidding team (bidder + teammate) have won >= bid amount. They are declared winners, else the defending team wins.

Each player from the winning team gets bid amount points.

Bid & won bonus: If the bidder wins, they get an additional 20 point bonus.

Whitewash bonus: If the bidder & teammate win all the 10 tricks, they get an additional 50 point bonus.
