## 🎨 **Color Scheme Analysis**

### **Primary Color Palette**

- **Gold**: `hsl(45 100% 60%)` - Primary accent, used for buttons, highlights, and important elements
- **Felt Green**: `hsl(140 35% 18%)` - Background and table felt
- **Casino Red**: `hsl(0 85% 55%)` - Accent color for destructive actions
- **Casino Black**: `hsl(0 0% 8%)` - Text on gold backgrounds
- **Casino White**: `hsl(0 0% 98%)` - Card backgrounds

### **Semantic Colors**

- **Primary**: Gold (`hsl(var(--gold))`)
- **Secondary**: Dark green (`hsl(140 25% 25%)`)
- **Muted**: Medium green (`hsl(140 20% 30%)`)
- **Accent**: Orange-red (`hsl(15 100% 55%)`)
- **Destructive**: Red (`hsl(0 84.2% 60.2%)`)

### **Card Colors**

- **Card Red**: `hsl(0 85% 55%)` - Hearts and diamonds
- **Card Black**: `hsl(0 0% 15%)` - Spades and clubs
- **Card Background**: `hsl(0 0% 98%)` - White card faces
